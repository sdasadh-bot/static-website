What is Cloud Computing?

Cloud computing is the on-demand delivery of computing resources such as storage, servers, databases, networking, and software over the internet. Instead of owning and maintaining physical infrastructure, users can access these resources as services, paying only for what they use. This model eliminates the need for upfront hardware investments and provides scalability, flexibility, and cost-efficiency.
